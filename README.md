# GameProjectS2-2026

ROUGH PLANNING DOC for story and game design.
Story overview

til I die....
this is a survival-horror exploration game about what’s left after the lights go out.


Post apocolyplic game based on if the Cold War had have gone nuclear
You wake up years after the event, alone, with no memory of how you got here. Then, one night, a radio crackles to life with a transmission from someone...or something.. You don’t know who’s sending it, or why but it’s the only thread leading anywhere, and so you follow it.


A desaturated, cold world grey-blue palettes tlou style, overcast light, and quiet that feels earned rather than empty. Most of the map shpuld be relitivly safe to walk, but abandoned. if you enter an irradiated zone, though it will get harder to survive, and darker.

gameplay

Real-time exploration through a maze of rooms and corridors, piecing together the source of the signal room by room. Most of the game is investigation, reading the environment, managing resources, deciding when it’s safe to push forward and when to turn back. Rooms that arent close to you, but on your screen will go black and stalkers can spawn in dark rooms. In irratiadted zones, the range of black rooms will increase, such to a point where the only lit up room could potentially be just the one you're in, and stalkers or other apocolyptic life could be spawning in adjacent tiles. radiation will also have a screen effect if you stay too long, and then eventually kill you. 

there will be two kinds of danger. 
Clean zones are explorable at your own pace. Irradiated zones demand a mask or suit, and every second inside costs you, your radiation bar climbs the longer you stay exposed.

Mobs.
rodents, deer and wild pigs, (all slightly affected by the radioation, think extra eyes or secondary limbs)

The Stalke is something that lives deep in the radiation. It doesn’t chase you through the whole map, it only exists where the fallout is worst, and it hunts by darkness and proximity. Get close in the dark without your torch and it attacks. torches have batteries that deplete, torch, your mask, your radiation exposure, all finite, all competing for attention while you try to investigate

there will be multiple ways to die.

stalked for too long.
envirmental death (holes or spikes)
death by radiation
death by enraging animals such as deers or boars.


Room-by-room discovery. maze of interconnected rooms and doors, built for exploration and tension rather than scale. rooms will be fairly large

Controls

WASD, Mouse for interact X for don/remove radiation mask or suit, Z for radio, F for flashlight, E for Unknown, esc for pause.
